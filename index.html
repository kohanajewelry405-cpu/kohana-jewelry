Our Story

Kohana means “little flower” in Japanese.

Inspired by the quiet beauty of flowers, each piece is designed to bring a small touch of elegance to everyday moments and special occasions.

Every piece is handmade by a Japanese artist using carefully selected materials sourced from Japan.

From everyday elegance to the stage and bridal moments, Kohana is jewelry made to become part of your story.
